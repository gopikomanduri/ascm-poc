## 💡 Pro Tips for LinkedIn

1. **Authentic founder voice** — Write as if Gopi is speaking (casual but professional)
2. **Problem-first messaging** — Lead with pain points, not features
3. **Social proof** — Include metrics, results, founder story
4. **Clear CTAs** — "Take the quiz" → "Launch campaign" → "Book demo"
5. **Consistent posting** — 3x/week minimum (LinkedIn algorithm favors consistency)
6. **Engage with community** — Comment on related posts about GTM, SaaS, AI
